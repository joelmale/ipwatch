DNS Leak Check

A VPN can carry your traffic correctly while your DNS lookups still go out through your ISP's resolvers instead of the tunnel — that leaks every domain you visit even though the connection looks fine. This on-demand test checks which resolvers your DNS queries actually reach and compares them against your VPN's exit network. It makes a few real requests to a third-party leak-test service and takes several seconds, so it only runs when you click.
